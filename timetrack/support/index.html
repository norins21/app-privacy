TimeTrack Support

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Developer: Noreens Obias (N.Obias)

TimeTrack is a personal time and mileage tracker. Start a one-tap timer, plan your day in time blocks, see your week and month at a glance, log trips by hand and export a CSV timesheet. There is no account and no sign-in.

How do I export my hours?

Open Settings → Export CSV and choose the current week or month. The file opens your phone’s share sheet, so you choose where it goes (email app, Files, Drive, etc.).

Are my entries backed up?

No. TimeTrack has no cloud sync and no servers, so there is no automatic backup. To keep a copy of your hours, export a CSV regularly and save it somewhere you choose.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you record stays on your phone, in a local database on your device. It is not sent to us.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Clear all data to wipe everything in the app, or simply uninstall TimeTrack: uninstalling removes the app and all of its data from your phone.
