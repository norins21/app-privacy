Space Tracky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Developer: Noreens Obias (N.Obias) · Package: com.noreensobias.spacetracky

Space Tracky is an informational companion for SpaceX / SPCX shareholders. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data on our servers for trading, brokerage, or advertising. Space Tracky is not a broker, does not execute trades, and does not provide investment advice.

Market, supply, and many figures are labelled DEMO until live adapters ship. Portfolio inputs and thesis watchlist preferences are stored only on your device. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this build.

Notifications

Notification permission is optional (for future launch or thesis alerts). Declining does not block the core informational UI.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your portfolio or thesis preferences with third parties for advertising.

Deletion

Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall to wipe on-device preferences.

Children

Space Tracky is not directed at children under 13.

Financial disclaimer

Educational / informational only. Nothing in the app is an offer to buy or sell securities.

Contact

Questions: contact Noreens Obias via the developer contact details on the Google Play listing for Space Tracky.
